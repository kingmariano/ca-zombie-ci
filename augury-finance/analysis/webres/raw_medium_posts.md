## 4. "IFO Post Mortem (Important Update)" — June 2021 (2 days after ~June 9-10)

> Yesterday while working with Polycat Finance, we discovered an error in the math of the IFO contract which prevented users from being able to withdraw OMEN tokens from the IFO Contract. As a response to this situation, Polycat Finance immediately deployed a new IFO Contract...
> Today... we further discovered that **we cannot get the old OMEN tokens off of the IFO Contract**... These are hardcoded in the IFO contract and because of the immutable nature of the blockchain, they cannot be modified. What this means to you is that since we cannot get the tokens off of the original contract we're going to need to **mint another 670,000 OMEN tokens** and send them to the current IFO contract...
> In technical terms, these OMEN tokens are **"dead"**. No one at Augury Finance or Polycat Finance has access to these OMEN tokens... They are completely unrecoverable.
> With this change, we will end up with **1,340,000 tokens minted for the Polycat IFO of which only 670,000 can be sold, ever.**
